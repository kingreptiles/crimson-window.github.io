Development status

Crimson Window is being developed as an AI-powered interactive storytelling and roleplay app. Descriptions on this website refer to current development builds and may change. No public release date, download, purchase, or particular future feature is promised here.

People with access to a development build may be shown separate account terms inside the app. These brief website notes do not replace those terms.

Current age rules

The current app requires users to be at least 16 to create an account. Mature content is restricted to adult accounts aged 18 or older and controlled through content preferences. Users aged 16–17 cannot enable it.

Accurate age information is required. Do not bypass age or access restrictions. “Mature content” does not mean that all content or behavior is permitted; see Community & safety.

AI and fictional content

AI responses can be inaccurate, incomplete, unexpected, or inconsistent with instructions. Fictional characters are not real people, and their output is not professional or emergency advice.

Review generated content before relying on it or sharing it. Available models, responses, cloud features, and message limits depend on the build, configuration, account access, and provider availability.

Sharing and other people’s rights

Use text, characters, and images only when you have the necessary rights or permission. A fan-made character is not a claim of endorsement by a franchise or rights holder.

Do not use the app to share unlawful material, exploit minors, distribute non-consensual intimate content, harass people, or invade someone’s privacy. Community content may be reported and reviewed using the app’s moderation tools.

Availability and payments

This website does not offer a paid subscription or collect payment details. Final plans, prices, seller information, purchase conditions, and service terms will be provided if and when a public paid service is offered.

If you use your own AI-provider account in a development build, that provider’s separate billing and rules apply.
