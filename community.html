Age and mature content

Account creation starts at 16. Users aged 16–17 cannot enable mature content. Lawful mature themes are restricted to adults aged 18 or older and controlled in Account → Settings → Content preferences.

Give accurate age information. Do not use another person’s account or attempt to bypass age restrictions. A mature-content setting does not override an AI provider’s policies.

Decide what you share

Current builds distinguish local creations, private uploads, and public Library content. Publishing a character or persona makes it available to other eligible users. Shared memories are posted copies of selected messages, not an automatic publication of every chat.

Check your rights to text and artwork before sharing them. Avoid posting personal information, private conversations involving other people, or material you do not have permission to use. A privacy or locked setting cannot prevent someone from copying content outside the app.

What crosses the line

  • Sexual content involving minors or anyone portrayed as a minor, grooming, or sexual exploitation.
  • Non-consensual intimate images of real people, impersonation intended to harm, doxxing, or stalking.
  • Credible threats, fraud, malicious software, or material intended to facilitate real-world harm.
  • Unlawful content or infringement of another person’s rights.
  • Attacks on the service or attempts to defeat access and safety controls.

Fictional adult themes do not authorize real-world abuse. AI output is fictional, and it is not evidence of a real person’s consent or views.

Report a concern

In a development build, open a character’s detail page and choose Report character. The app attaches the character’s identity. The reporting flow can also open the backend’s web reporting page, which does not require an account.

If you cannot use that flow, email uta.cornelc@gmail.com. Include the character or content location, what concerns you, and enough information to understand the issue. Do not send illegal images, passwords, or API keys.

The public backend reporting address will be listed when a public service is available. We are not publishing a local-development server address here.

Review and creator appeals

The current moderation tools support notices, review, reasoned decisions, restrictions, and creator appeals. A report does not automatically remove a character. Restrictions follow a moderation decision; edits alone do not lift one.

Creators can find decisions about their content in Moderation decisions from the main menu and submit an appeal where offered. A pending appeal does not automatically restore access.

Existing local files and cached copies cannot always be recalled after publication or restriction. These development notes describe available tools; they are not a claim of legal certification or a promised review deadline.

Fan-made characters

A fan-made character does not imply that Crimson Window is endorsed by or affiliated with its rights holder. Creators remain responsible for permissions, and a disclaimer does not override someone else’s rights.
