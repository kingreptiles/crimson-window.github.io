01 / Make the story yours

There’s more to a conversation
than the next message.

Crimson Window brings the cast, your role, and the way the story is told into the same space.

01

A cast you can create.

Define characters with introductions, scenarios, opening messages, artwork, and tags. Keep creations local, upload privately, or publish eligible characters to the Library.

02

A voice you can shape.

Personas guide a character’s behavior and narrative style. Your profiles describe who you are playing, while custom instructions set the boundaries of your role.

03

A story you can revisit.

Pin important messages, maintain continuity summaries, explore alternative responses, restore checkpoints, or let a character continue while you remain silent.

04

A moment you can share.

Turn selected conversation messages into a shared memory. Edit the copy before publishing, revisit your posts, and react to community memories.

02 / Your way into the scene

Choose the voice.
Keep your agency.

Switch AI packages, tune your preferred response length, and choose how your chat looks. Current builds also support a user-owned OpenAI API connection.

{user} becomes your profile name.
